A sample answer

The situation: I have 10 minutes with our VP and the leadership team on Thursday to present Q3 results. Revenue beat target by 8% but churn went up from 4% to 6%. The VP cuts people off after two minutes. Goal: approve one more headcount.
Your Next Action

Present mixed Q3 results to the VP

What I'd do

Open with one sentence that covers both numbers and your ask, then use the rest of the 10 minutes to show the churn rise is understood and how the new headcount fixes it.

Why

  • Your VP cuts people off after two minutes. If the result and the ask come in the first 30 seconds, you've made your point even if you're interrupted.

  • Beat target, churn up, what do we do, add one person: that order puts the bad news in context and leads straight to the ask.

  • "Churn went from 4% to 6%" is easy to shrug off. Saying how many customers or how much revenue that 2% means makes the case for headcount land.

How to do it

  1. Today, turn the churn rise into a number people feel, such as [X] customers or [$] a year, and write your first sentence with it.
  2. Before Thursday, cut the deck to three sections (revenue, churn, the plan) and put the churn cause and fix on one slide.
  3. In the first minute on Thursday, say the opening below, then ask if they want the churn detail or the plan first.

What to say

Open with

Q3 revenue beat target by 8%. Churn went up from 4% to 6%, which is about [X] customers, and we know why: [cause]. I'm asking for one more person in Q4 to fix it. That person would [task], which should bring churn back to [target] by [date]. I'll show the three numbers behind this, then the plan.
