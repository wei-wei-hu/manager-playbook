02   You get Your Next Action

Take on a new project when your plate is full

What I'd do

Say yes to the project, and ask your manager which current task should move to make room.

Why

  • Your manager may not know how full your week is. Showing your list lets them choose what moves, so you don't end up working late.
  • Sorting your tasks first shows which ones can wait or go to someone else, so the talk with your manager is short.

How to do it

  1. Today, list what you're working on and when each task is due.
  2. Mark one or two tasks that could wait or be handed off.
  3. Tomorrow, show your manager the list and ask which one should move.

What to say

Say this to your manager

I'm happy to take on the new project. Right now I have the Friday report and two other tasks. To do the new project well, one of these needs to move. Which one would you like me to push back?
