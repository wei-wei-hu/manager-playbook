## What it is

Underline AI is your personalized AI advisor. Bring a question, a challenge, or a decision you're stuck on, and get advice that fits your situation, all on one page.

Underline AI draws from a carefully curated library on communication, influence, negotiation, leadership, and getting work done. It brings together the most useful thinking for your situation and turns it into practical advice. You'll see what to do, what to say, and the ideas behind it.

**Topics:** Career, Communication, Leadership, Productivity, Decisions, Work-life balance.

[![Underline AI landing page on a laptop](docs/underline-laptop.png)](https://wei-wei-hu.github.io/underline-ai/)

## How it works

1. **Share your situation.** What's happening, what you want to happen, and anything else that matters.
2. **Connect the ideas.** Underline AI searches its curated library, brings together the ideas that fit your situation, and turns them into practical advice.
3. **Leave with Your Next Action.** What to do, what to say, your next steps, and the ideas behind the advice.

## Your Next Action

Every answer has the same five parts:

- **What I'd do:** the one most valuable, decisive recommendation for your situation
- **What I'd say:** the actual words to use, in a warm, human tone
- **Do this next:** three to five actions, drawn from the library and adapted to you
- **Watch out:** one trap the sources warn you to avoid
- **Underlined:** the three to five ideas behind the advice, in the authors' own words where possible, with their sources

## Take it with you

Under every answer:

- **Email it:** sends the answer to your inbox, designed like the page
- **Save as PDF** and **Print:** a clean one-page version with the Underline AI design
- **Copy:** the answer as plain text

Free answers also ask **Was this helpful?** One tap on 👍 or 👎 sends the feedback, and you can add which part didn't work and a comment.
