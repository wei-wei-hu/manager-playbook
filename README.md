## Why Underline AI?

**1.** Sometimes, we face situations where we don't know the best way to respond.

`How do I handle this?` `How do I get buy-in?` `What do I tell my manager?` `How do I deal with them?`

**2.** Underline isn't trying to know everything. AI helps connect the thinking and apply it to your specific situation.

`Communication` `Influence` `Career` `Leadership` `Decisions` `Productivity` `Relationships` `Life` `+ more`

**3.** Underline helps you find a better way to handle this than you would have thought of yourself. It recommends your next best action:

`What I'd do` `What I'd say` `Do this next` `Watch out` `Underlined`

## How it works

1. **Share your situation.** What's happening, what you want to happen, and anything else that matters.
2. **Connect the ideas.** Underline AI searches and brings together the best ideas that fit your situation, and turns them into practical advice.
3. **Leave with Your Next Action.** What to do, what to say, your next steps, and the ideas behind the advice.
